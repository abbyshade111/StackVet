# What is in `docs/`

Every document in this folder, what it is for, who reads it, and whether it is **live** (kept true as `sv` changes),
**dated** (a reading made on a day, kept as it was written), or **generated** (written by a tool; never edited by
hand). Where to start, by who you are, is the reading order in the project's
[`README.md`](../README.md#reading-order). Backlog 0441 made this index, on 14 October 2026.

## For the person building an app

| Document | What it is for | Kind |
|---|---|---|
| [`GETTING-STARTED.md`](GETTING-STARTED.md) | The guide: a first afternoon with StackVet, step by step. | live |
| [`INSTALL.md`](INSTALL.md) | Installing StackVet on your own computer for `sv report --run`, an image of your own, and keeping it up to date. | live |
| [`ALL-YOUR-APPS.md`](ALL-YOUR-APPS.md) | The page that shows all your apps, and what changed between checks. | live |
| [`KNOWN-VULNERABILITIES.md`](KNOWN-VULNERABILITIES.md) | Checking the app's packages against known vulnerabilities, with data you download. | live |
| [`REVIEW.md`](REVIEW.md) | Recording your own word with `sv review`, and the seals that keep it. | live |
| [`PROMPTS.md`](PROMPTS.md) | Prompts to paste into your AI coding tool, each tried with and without. | live |
| [`GLOSSARY.md`](GLOSSARY.md) | The words StackVet uses, each in a sentence or two. | live |

## Dated readings, in [`research/`](research/)

Each was made on a day, at the owner's asking, and is kept as it was written; a note at its top says when, and where
what has changed since is written down. Its old path in `docs/` holds a one-line pointer, so what cites the old path
still leads to it.

| Document | What it is for | Kind |
|---|---|---|
