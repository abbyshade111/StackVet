The project against the OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications, by period

The figure of the cut-off (figure-agentic.html) marked incidents, defenses, open items, and sv's checks against the ten agentic risks, from all three lenses, to 4 October. Here the incidents are placed in the period their record dates them to. Defenses and checks are standing files more often than events; one whose record names a pull request or commit is dated by it, and the rest are counted as not dated. No incident since the cut-off has been placed against the agentic list: the faults ledger since says what kind each fault is, not which agentic risk, so the StackVet period has no panel of its own here. A period is a span of time, not a version: sv's rows from before its promotion on 26 September, when it was built in agnostic/ beside v1, are in version 1's period.
