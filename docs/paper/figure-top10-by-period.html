The project's security faults against the OWASP Top 10:2025, by period

The figure of the cut-off (figure-top10.html) placed each weakness in StackVet's own code, and each verdict that failed open, through a CWE on the category's 2025 list, to 4 October. Here those same rows, each placed in the period its record dates it to. Nothing found since the cut-off has been placed through a CWE: the faults ledger since has a kind for each fault, not a CWE, so the StackVet period after 4 October is a count of the ledger's security and fail-open faults, without a category. Placing them is a reading of each fault, not done here. A period is a span of time, not a version: sv's rows from before its promotion on 26 September, when it was built in agnostic/ beside v1, are in version 1's period.
