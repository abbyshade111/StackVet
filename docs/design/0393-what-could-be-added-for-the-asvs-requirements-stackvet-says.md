# What could be added for the ASVS requirements StackVet says nothing about (20 October 2026)

Written 20 October 2026, at the owner's request, for the 103 ASVS 5.0 requirements whose row in docs/REQUIREMENTS.md said *No check* and that had no design question, security-notes section, or by-hand instruction either (backlog 0624 relabels them; backlog 0625 holds the bigger builds). Each requirement's text was read in `data/frameworks/asvs-5.0.0.json`. These are proposals, not built work: nothing here changes a report until it is built, tested by breaking the thing it guards, and its citations are regenerated with `python3 tools/coverage.py`.

**How to read the tiers.** *Checked in part* is the most a probe or settings check here can reach: each tries part of what its requirement asks. *Needs attention only* means the check can show the control missing, but a clean run credits nothing. *Documented*, *checked by hand*, and *attested* are the owner's, recorded through `sv review`, and are never *checked*. No proposal needs `sv` to open a new network connection: every probe goes to the app `sv` started in its own container, through the probe sidecar it already uses, and the OAuth and OpenID ones talk to `sv`'s own stand-in provider or to the app's own server.

**Two things to know first.**

- 71 of the 103 are level 3. `sv` holds every app to level 1 or 2 today (`target_level` returns 1 or 2); level 3 is the open backlog item 0507. Until that lands, only the 32 at level 1 or 2 can appear in any report, so they come first in the lists at the end.
- Many already have a rule that limits them to the apps that have the thing: V10.4, V10.6 and V10.7 (`authorization-server`), V17 (`webrtc`), V1.2.8 (`latex`), V1.3.9 (`memcache`), V1.4 (`unmanaged-code`), V6.6.1 (`out-of-band-auth`), V6.8.3 (`saml`), V6.8 (`oauth`). For those, the proposal is about what to check once the requirement applies.

**Problems found along the way.**

- `data/knowledge/applicability.json` marks V15.4 `never`, with the reason "level 3 only, which is above the level this app targets". `applicability-v2.json` keeps it on purpose (`real_data.rs` says the reason is honest). It stops being honest the day an app is held to level 3: V15.4.1 to V15.4.4 would show as not applying, with a reason that is false. The same holds for C5.1. Backlog 0507 should replace both rules.
- Of the 16 tagged "asks for documentation", 9 do ask for a document (V3.1.1, V8.1.3, V8.1.4, V11.1.4, V13.1.2, V13.1.3, V13.1.4, V15.1.4, V15.1.5). The other 7 (V3.2.3, V3.5.8, V3.7.5, V8.2.4, V13.2.6, V13.3.4, V15.2.5) ask for the app to *behave* as documented, or for a coding practice. A notes section would be the wrong tool for those, so they get a design question, a by-hand instruction, a probe, or a static check here. `security-notes.json`'s `elsewhere` list covers only levels 1 and 2 today, so none of the 9 was ever considered.
- V6.6.4 (push prompts), section V6.7 (cryptographic sign-in), and V12.3.5 (traffic between the app's own services) apply to apps that cannot have the thing they ask about. Each is a one-line applicability fix, listed under quick wins.
