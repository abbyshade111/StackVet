# ADR-101: The phone, two-factor, and recovery changes beside the email change, and the owner's word on which the app offers

**Status:** proposed, 12 October 2026, in the claim of backlog 0232, part 7 (group G). The owner's word the same day:
add the settings, with a way to say which changes the app does not offer, and "include a way for the owner to confirm
what is offered and what isn't in sv review".

**Governs:** `crates/sv-manifest/src/lib.rs` (the settings), `crates/sv-check/src/signed_in/passwords.rs` (the check),
and `crates/sv-cli/src/review.rs` (the owner's confirmation). A pull request that changes what these say about V7.5.1
changes this record too, or says in its description why it does not (`docs/adr/README.md`).

## Context

V7.5.1 asks that a person sign in again (give their password) before changing an email address, a phone number, the
two-factor settings, or other information used to recover the account. `sv` tries one of the four, the email change
(`change-email`, backlog 0244), and credits V7.5.1 in part, since one change cannot speak for the others (ADR-053,
Later). Most small apps have no phone number or two-factor at all, and `sv` cannot tell from outside which of the
changes an app offers.

## Decision (proposed)

- **Three settings beside `change-email`:** `change-phone`, `change-two-factor`, and `change-recovery`, each written as
  `change-email` is, with `{password}` for the current password. Each is used only on an account made for it through
  `signup`, never on the two test users, and judged as the email change is: the change sent without the password must
  be refused.
- **What the app does not offer:** `not-offered = ["phone", "two-factor"]` beside them, naming changes the app has no
  way to make. A name that is not one of the three is refused, as is a name that is also given a setting.
- **The owner confirms it in `sv review`.** The list counts only once a person has confirmed it in `sv review`, which
  seals it as the scope answers are sealed (ADR-024, Later). Until then the report shows it as the AI coding tool's word
  and the credit stays in part. The confirmation is the owner's word, and the report says so: `sv` does not check that
  an app has no phone change.
- **When the credit is whole.** V7.5.1 is credited whole only when the email change was tried and refused without the
  password, and each of the other three was either tried and refused or is confirmed as not offered. Otherwise it stays
  in part, naming what was not tried.
- **Not in this record.** The password checks tried at a password change as well as sign-up stay in part either way
  (ADR-090: one password is one sample of every password), and TOTP beside an emailed code (V6.5.1, V6.5.5) waits for a
  change of its own; both stay in group G's remainder.

## Consequences

- An app with only an email change can earn V7.5.1 whole, on the owner's confirmed word that it has nothing else.
- A tool that writes `not-offered` alone cannot earn the credit; the owner's confirmation is what moves it.
- An owner who confirms wrongly gives the report a false "whole"; the report names the confirmation as theirs.
