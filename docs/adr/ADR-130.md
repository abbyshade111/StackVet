# ADR-130: Level 3 is the owner's opt-in, `[app] level = 3`, and it only ever raises the level

**Status:** accepted, 20 October 2026, in the pull request that builds it; proposed the same day with the claim for
backlog 0507, at the owner's go of that day ("enable level 3 upon user request").

**Governs:** `crates/sv-manifest/src/lib.rs::target_level`, `crates/sv-manifest/src/lib.rs::answers_level`,
and `crates/sv-check/src/seal.rs::scope_review_fields`; and the rules for V15.4 and C5.1 in the applicability overlay
(data/applicability-v2.json, which ADR-015 governs).
A pull request that changes these changes this record too, or says in its description why it does not
(`docs/adr/README.md`).

## Context

`sv` held every app to ASVS level 1 or 2, from two answers in `stackvet.toml`: who uses the app (`[app] audience`) and
what it holds about people (`[data] categories`), unanswered being level 2 (ADR-024). Level 3 is about ninety more ASVS
requirements and sixty-one more of AISVS, and no app could be held to them. That was held back until the questions
level 3 asks could be answered (backlog 0507); with 0624's relabelling, an owner holding an app to level 3 sees, for
each requirement, whether a check speaks to it, whether StackVet asks the owner, or that nothing does yet, and the owner
asked for it.

## Decision

- **The answer is `level = 3` under `[app]`, and it only ever raises the level.** `target_level` is the higher of what
  the two answers set (`answers_level`, unchanged) and the level asked for. Under ADR-015 the owner's word adds
  requirements and never removes one, so a level below what the answers set (`level = 1` on an app its customers use)
  is refused when the file is read, naming the answers that set the higher one and saying how to change it; a value
  that is not 1, 2, or 3 is refused too. `level = 2` on a level-1 app raises it as well; on a level-2 app it changes
  nothing.
- **It is confirmed with the other two.** `sv review`'s level confirmation shows `[app] level` when it is there, and
  `[scope-review]` records it. Its seal is made over `level=N` after the audience when the level was asked for, and over
  nothing more when it was not, so a confirmation sealed before this record still holds. Adding, changing, or removing
  the level after confirming makes the answers unconfirmed again, as changing either of the other two does.
- **What the report says:** the level, with why ("stackvet.toml asks for level 3 (`[app] level`), above the level 2
  its other answers set"), and every requirement at or below it, decided by the same applicability rules.
- **Three things that assumed the top level is 2,** fixed: the `level2` condition was the target being exactly 2, and
  is now 2 or more, or a level-3 app would be told a level-2 requirement does not apply; the consistency check's "already
  assessed at level 2" says the level the app is at; and V15.4's and C5.1's `never` rules, whose reason ("level 3 only,
  above the level this app targets") became false, are replaced in the overlay. V15.4 is `always`. C5.1 turns on what
  the AI does: C5.1.1 (step-up sign-in for model deployment, weight export, and training data) on `self-hosted-model`
  or `training`, and C5.1.2 (agents authenticating to each other) on `multi-agent`. Below level 3 all six are above the
  app's level, which the report says, and never "not applicable".

## Alternatives considered

- **Let `level` set the level either way.** An owner could then lower an app its customers use to level 1 by writing
  one line, and the requirements level 2 adds would be reported as above its level. That is the wrong "no" ADR-015
  exists to stop; the answers that set the level are what to change, and the refusal says so.
- **A new condition, `level3`, for V15.4 and C5.1.** Not needed: a requirement above the app's level is already set
  apart before any rule is read, so `always` is true at every level.

## Consequences

- No report changes for an app that does not ask: `target_level` is what it was, and every existing confirmation holds.
- The level-3 additions moved to 0507 from 0624 (the catalogs opened to level 3 and their entries, the level-3
  locators, V11.2.5's citation, and the cookie-size check) can now matter, and follow in pull requests of their own.
- `docs/COVERAGE.md` and the two-level figures the paper and the website quote count every requirement, as before, and
  do not change.
- Held by `crates/sv-manifest/tests/level_opt_in.rs` (raised, refused below the answers and outside 1 to 3, `level2`
  true at 3), `crates/sv-check/tests/scope_review_level.rs` (the seal unchanged without the key, the level sealed after
  the audience, and a confirmation holding only for the level it confirmed), and
  `crates/sv-frameworks/tests/level_three.rs` (above the level below 3, applying at 3 by their conditions). With
  `target_level` ignoring the key, the refusal removed, the seal adding a level field always, and the V15.4 rule
  removed, each failed.
