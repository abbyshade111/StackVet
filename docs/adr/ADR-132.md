# ADR-132: stackvet.dev is built from the repository first, and where it is hosted is chosen after

**Status:** accepted, 22 October 2026, in the pull request that builds the site; proposed the same day with the
claim for backlog 0376, at the owner's answer of that day, asked in a session where stackvet.dev should be hosted:
"Build first then do some research and provide up to 5 options, a mix of free and low cost, with the pros and cons
and your recommendation".

**Governs:** `tools/website.py`, `crates/sv-cli/tests/website.rs`, `docs/website/self-assessment/README.md`, and the
repository's own `stackvet.toml` (what StackVet's report on itself is held to). A pull request that changes what the
site is built from, where it is written, or that nothing of it is published without the owner's word, changes this
record too, or says in its description why it does not (`docs/adr/README.md`).

## Context

The home page's words and look were settled on 12 October 2026 (`docs/website/home-page.md`, backlog 0224), and the
framework pages are written by `tools/framework_pages.py` into `exports/site/` (ADR-129). Every condition 0376 set for
building is met; going live still waits for the walk-through (backlog 0373) and the owner's word. Where the site is
hosted, and how stackvet.dev and stackvet.app point at it, are the owner's: hosting may cost money.

## Decision

- **Built first, by a script in the repository,** with no dependency beyond Python's own library: the home page, the
  credits page, How StackVet works, StackVet checks itself, and the reports page, beside the framework pages, into
  `exports/site/`, which git ignores, as `tools/framework_pages.py` writes.
- **Read, never copied,** as ADR-129 holds the framework pages: the words from `docs/website/home-page.md` and
  `docs/website/reports-page.md`, the coverage numbers from `docs/COVERAGE.md`, the credits from the licenses the
  repository records, and the self-assessment and its bill of materials from where they are kept.
- **Self-contained:** no script, style sheet, font, or image is fetched from anywhere; the type falls back to the
  system's when Atkinson Hyperlegible is not installed.
- **Nothing is published** by building it. Hosting is chosen by the owner from up to five options, free and low cost,
  each with its pros and cons and a recommendation, researched after the site is built and recorded here when chosen.

## Where the build differs

- **The self-assessment is a new one, made for the site,** at the owner's word on 22 October 2026 ("the self
  assessment should probably be updated", and "We could test level 3 that way, too"). The paper's, of 4 October 2026
  (`docs/paper/self-assessment-v2`), stays as it was, since the paper cites it. The new one is `sv report .` on this
  repository with `[app] level = 3` added to its `stackvet.toml` (ADR-130), and is kept in
  `docs/website/self-assessment/`, which says how it was made.
- **Only its small files are kept,** the owner's choice of the same day, since nothing committed is ever removed:
  the folder `sv` wrote was 41 MB, most of it the findings in test and sample code, and the eight files kept are
  about 1.1 MB. The page links them, and says the rest was left out.
- **It is shown as it came out:** 312 things to fix, most of them, by the look of them, StackVet's rules firing on its
  own detection code. The owner chose to build the page now and go through them in an item of their own (backlog
  0633), with nothing set aside until the owner confirms it with `sv review`.
- **The words are read, and the notes on them left out:** a home page section's last list is its notes, and a section
  holding only a line in italics is a placeholder; neither is shown. The coverage numbers the words were written with
  are replaced by `docs/COVERAGE.md`'s, and the build stops if the sentence a number sits in has changed. The AI
  tools named in "Get started" are read from `docs/prompts/setup.md`'s "Tried so far". A button for something not yet
  made (the walk-through, backlog 0373) is shown as coming.
- **How StackVet works** is the paper's description of 4 October 2026, said to be that, with the current drawing of
  the five steps (`docs/website/diagrams/how-it-works.svg`). The inventory of what a run gathers, which the owner
  named for it on 12 October 2026, was a page in a session and is not in the repository.
- **The framework pages** are written into `standards/` (ADR-129, Later).
- **Held by** `crates/sv-cli/tests/website.rs`. With the notes kept, the placeholder shown, the report files not
  copied, or any folder written over, it fails. With the numbers not filled in, only the script's self-test fails,
  since the figures written in the words are today's.
