# ADR-120: The guide's word budget is 4,500

**Status:** accepted. Decided by the owner on 15 October 2026 ("let's raise the limit to 4,500"), after the session
building the three-tool prompt trial's wording items asked whether to keep the 4,200-word budget or raise it.

**Governs:** `crates/sv-cli/tests/guide_first_afternoon.rs`. A pull request that changes the guide's word budget there
changes this record too, or says in its description why it does not (`docs/adr/README.md`).

## Context

Backlog 0440 split `docs/GETTING-STARTED.md` on 14 October 2026: the guide kept the first afternoon, under 4,000 words
then, and installing, several apps, and the advisory downloads moved to pages of their own. The test held it to 4,200
words, so the guide could not grow back into three readers' material.

The trial of 14 October 2026 (`docs/research/SETUP-PROMPT-TRIALS-2026-10-14.md`) queued items that add sentences to
the build prompt (backlog 0545 to 0550), which the guide carries word for word as its step 4, and to its "Tried so far"
paragraph (0551). By 15 October the guide stood at about 4,197 words. Each new sentence had been paid for by shortening
another, some of them in steps a first-time reader needs.

## Decision

- **The budget is 4,500 words**, counted as before (`split_whitespace`).
- **What the guide holds does not change.** The pages the test requires it to link, and the later-day material it
  must not carry, stay as they are; the budget only gives the build prompt and the trial notes room.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep 4,200 and go on shortening.** Each prompt sentence a trial shows is needed would cost a sentence of
  explanation elsewhere, for a reader who is not a programmer.
- **No budget.** Nothing would stop the guide from growing back into the 6,300-word file 0440 split.

## Consequences

- About 300 words of room at the time of the decision. The next trial's items fit in it; a later one may bring the
  question back, and it is the owner's again.

## How it is held

`the_guide_keeps_the_first_afternoon_and_links_the_later_days` in `crates/sv-cli/tests/guide_first_afternoon.rs`.
